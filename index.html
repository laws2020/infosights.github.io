About

Lawrence Daniel
Garba

I'm the founder and lead consultant of Infosights Consulting — an independent practice at the intersection of open-source R tooling and African economic data.

My work addresses a structural gap: Africa's public institutions generate substantial economic and statistical data, but that data remains largely inaccessible to researchers and analysts who need it in programmatic, reproducible form. I build the infrastructure that closes that gap.

I bring deep R programming expertise — favouring base R's granular control and aesthetic rigour over abstracted libraries — and a practitioner's understanding of Nigerian and African financial systems, tax policy, and development data.

African data exists. The plumbing to access it programmatically does not — yet. I'm building it, one package at a time, MIT licensed and freely available to every researcher on the continent.
